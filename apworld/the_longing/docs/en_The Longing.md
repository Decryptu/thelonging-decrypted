# The Longing

*The Longing* is a slow-burn subterranean exploration game where the Shade waits 400 days to wake the sleeping King.

## What is randomized?

- **Mattocks, Secret Key, and Cavern Passes** for breaking weak walls, mining crystal veins, and unlocking the Library, Mystery Rooms, and Upper Cavern Gate.
- **Progressive Walk Speed** begins at the vanilla pace. Four upgrades raise it to 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75×, and 2×.
- **Progressive Waypoint Recall** lets Shade return to saved waypoints. Open the game's waypoint panel to see your current pace and recall ring. Click to walk as usual, or Shift-click **Go** or **Home** to recall there. Three upgrades cover the inner kingdom, the middle caverns and Library, then the deep and upper caverns. Visit and mark a waypoint first. Home can be recalled after your first visit.
- **Classic Literature Books** across the Library, Secret Tower, Entrance Hall, and Mystery Rooms.
- **Cavern Crystals** (White, Rose, Blue, Purple, Black, and Giant Crystals).
- **Moss & Giant Mushroom Spores** for building the Shade's bed and ascending to the Secret Tower and Well.
- **Home Comforts, Relics, Musical Instrument Parts, and Hearth Fuel** (coal, timber, flint, sulfur).
- **Time Skips & Time Rate Boosts** to pace the 400-day countdown for a real multiworld session.

- **The Cavern Peddler** keeps a stall in the Pillared Gallery. Walk up and talk to trade coal for eight multiworld checks, relics, hats, companions, and small favors. Coal drops from the ceiling beside the stall. The last four checks need a Mattock.
- **Relics** sit in the bottom of the game's frame. Click one to use it and hover over it to read about it. Each needs a real-time rest afterwards that continues while the game is closed. Another copy readies it and shortens its rest.
- **Hats and companions** appear in the sides of the frame. Click to put one on or take it off.
- **Everything else you own** (walking pace, recall ring, time rate, passes, coal, moss, and so on) is shown along the bottom of the frame.

## Goals

- **Wake the King:** Reduce the countdown to zero and wake the King in his chamber.
- **Escape to the Surface:** Climb to the Well and ascend to the Surface.
- **Secret Ending:** Unlock and complete the hidden ending atop the Secret Tower.
- **Crystal & Book Hunt:** Collect the required Crystals and Classic Books and return to the Shade's Home.
- **Any Ending:** Complete any of the ending conditions above.
